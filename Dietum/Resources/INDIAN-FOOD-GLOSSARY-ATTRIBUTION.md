# Indian Food Glossary Attribution

The Romanized Indian food aliases in `IndianFoodGlossary.swift` were derived
from the Hindi-to-English food glossary published by MyIndianStove:

https://myindianstove.com/indian-food-glossary/

Dietum stores short ingredient lookup aliases only. It does not copy the
source page's cooking explanations or recipe content. Nutrition values still
come from the bundled Indian Nutrient Databank or USDA FoodData Central.
